Olympiad and Contest Prep

Our signature and most storied program, FIZIKA's Olympiad Training has inspired countless students to seek the thrill of problem-solving.

2025

Virtual SPhO lessons

From June to October 2025, we ran a weekly online class preparing around 15 students for the Singapore Physics Olympiad — 16 live Zoom sessions in all. We recorded and published every lesson to YouTube so anyone could follow along, and wrote a full set of original handouts, each with illustrative worked examples and problem sets.

Watch the recordings on YouTube → See the handouts →

2026

SJPO at Peirce Secondary School

In Jan 2026, we partnered with Peirce Secondary School to bring free and effective SJPO training workshops to their students. Together, we ran a 5-session Mechanics crash course for around 20 students. Over the sessions, the students were exposed to many different problem types they had never encoutered, and learnt to apply mathematics and physical principles to real problems.

2026

SJPO at Science Center Singapore

Running concurrently with the Peirce programme, we worked with Science Centre Singapore to deliver 10 weekly SJPO classes to about 20 students drawn from schools across the island including PLMGSS, NJC, Greendale Secondary, CHIJ, and more. To support them, we wrote a comprehensive SJPO handout set, complete with worked examples and practice problems. The sessions were highly interactive, with plenty of opportunities for students to ask questions and engage directly with the trainers!
