01 — Our mission

Curiosity shouldn’t depend on a price tag.

Too many bright, curious students hit a wall the moment enrichment carries a fee — the prep course, the tutor, the competition registration. Project FIZIKA exists to dismantle that wall. We’re a team of high-school and university students who believe rigorous STEM learning should be a public good, not a private purchase.

We design our own handouts, write full worked solutions, and run live sessions where asking “wait, why?” is the whole point. Everything we make is free to use, share, and build on — because the fastest way to grow the next generation of scientists is to stop making them pay for the door.

03 — How we teach

Four principles behind everything we make.

I.

Intuition before formulas

We start with the picture and the “why,” then earn the equation. Memorizing comes last, if at all.

II.

Free, forever

No paywalls, no “premium” tier. If we make it, you can have it — and so can your whole class.

III.

Near-peer mentorship

Our teachers were in your seat last year. They remember exactly where it gets confusing.

IV.

Show the full working

Every solution we publish is complete and honest — no “it can be shown that.” You see every step.
