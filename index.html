01 — Our mission

Curiosity shouldn’t depend on a price tag.

Although Singapore's education system is highly regarded for good reason, two things shape what that experience really looks like day to day. First, good STEM mentorship isn't spread evenly: a handful of schools have built up the culture, training pathways, and learning resources needed to take a student from curious to competitive, while many other students just don't have that same access. Second, because exams carry so much weight in our kiasu culture, learning becomes more like a chore than something to be appreciated.

Our project hopes to bring STEM enrichment focused on Physics to students who haven't had the same kind of access to it as we did, and create space for learning driven by curiosity rather than tests -- all without a price tag.

02 — Our Core Values

Four guiding principles.

I.

Collaboration over competition.

We believe in sharing knowledge and resources, fostering a community where everyone can learn from each other.

II.

Student focused, always.

Student needs and feedback will remain our first priority.

III.

Original and effective content.

When preparing our materials, we think back to the challenges we encountered back in JC, ensuring what we write is useful and engaging.

IV.

Professionalism.

Every workshop, talk, or activity is delivered competently and respectfully.
