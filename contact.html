FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still stuck? The general inbox is always open.

Is everything really free?

Yes — every program, workshop, mentor match, and resource is completely free. We’re a volunteer-run initiative and we intend to keep it that way.

Who can join your programs?

Any student curious about STEM. Most participants are in grades 8–12, but our resource library and many sessions are open to everyone, regardless of background or prior experience.

I’m a student — can I volunteer to teach?

Absolutely — near-peer teaching is the heart of what we do. Email hello@projectfizika.org with a sentence or two about what you’d love to help with.

Can my school or club partner with FIZIKA?

We’d love that. We run workshops, talks, and club support for partner schools. Reach out to hello@projectfizika.org and we’ll find a time to talk.

Can I use your handouts in my classroom?

Please do. Our resources are released under CC BY-NC 4.0 — free to share and adapt for non-commercial use, with credit to Project FIZIKA.
