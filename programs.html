Four ways we make STEM click.

Every program is free, student-led, and open to anyone willing to be curious. Whether you want a shot at a national olympiad, a hands-on workshop, or just a great PDF at 11 pm before a test — start here.

Program 01

Olympiad & Contest Prep

Seasonal cohorts that take competition math and physics seriously — without the gatekeeping. Past-paper drills, strategy clinics, and real mentor feedback on your full written solutions.

Getting started

Three steps, no paperwork.

  1. 01

    Pick a program

    Browse the four above and find the one that fits how you want to learn. Not sure? Email us — we’ll help.

  2. 02

    Say hello

    Send a quick note through our contact page. There’s no application and no cost — just an introduction.

  3. 03

    Show up curious

    We’ll get you a schedule, a seat, or a stack of resources. Bring your questions — that’s the whole point.
